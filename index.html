Organ Functions

Uterus: A pear-shaped organ where a baby can grow...

Ovaries: Small organs that store eggs and produce hormones...

Fallopian Tubes: Tubes that connect the ovaries to the uterus...

Cervix: Lower part of the uterus connecting to the vagina...

Menstrual Cycle

The menstrual cycle is typically 28 days but can range 21–35 days...

  • Day 1–5: Menstruation (bleeding)
  • Day 6–14: Uterine lining builds
  • Day 14: Ovulation (egg released)
  • Day 15–28: Lining thickens, hormones change

Hormones in the Menstrual Cycle

Estrogen: Builds uterine lining.

Progesterone: Maintains lining after ovulation.

FSH: Stimulates egg growth.

LH: Triggers ovulation.

Questions & Answers

Answered by pediatrician Dr. Vani Venkatachalam of Care & Cure Pediatrics.

1. Why are my periods so different from my friends?

Every girl's body is different... excessive menstrual bleeding.

2. Is it bad if I skip a month sometimes?

Period skipping is normal... wouldn’t hurt to see a physician.

3. Why does my mood get really bad before my period?

This is probably PMS... could be PMDD which is treatable.

4. I feel super tired during my period. Is that normal?

Yes, that is normal... physician might check iron status.

5. Sometimes I get clumps or clots in my blood—is that bad?

Small clots can be normal... excessive bleeding requires doctor visit.

6. Am I able to swim or participate in sports on my period?

Yes, you shouldn't let your period hold you back...

7. Why am I sometimes left with an odour when I am on my period?

Some smell is okay... consult a doctor if strong/pungent.

8. Is it okay to be embarrassed to have my period?

Totally normal, but you don’t have to be...

9. What do I do if I don't have any pads or tampons—what can I use?

Use clean cloths... reuse safely with soap/water and sun-drying.

10. How do I stay clean if I cannot shower every day?

Gently wash your private area... breathable underwear helps.

11. How do I keep the same cloth or pad on all day?

Change every 6–8 hours... prolonged use risks infection.

12. What do I do if I lack products or am too shy to ask?

Free products available... seek help from teacher/nurse/adult.

13. Why do my cramps worsen some months?

Cramps = uterus contractions... heat packs + exercise help.

14. Why do I get bloated or puffy before my period?

Hormonal changes cause water retention... drink water, avoid salty foods.

15. What do I do if my period bleeds through my clothes at school?

Happens to everyone... tie sweatshirt, bring extra underwear.

16. Is it wrong that I use toilet paper instead of a pad for a while?

Short-term solution only... switch to proper pad ASAP.

17. What if I don't have a location where I can change my pad?

Find temporary private places... even ask a friend to stand guard.

18. How do I avoid rashes when changing isn't an option?

Use petroleum jelly... loose clothes help air circulation.

19. What if I can't wash my hands before/after changing?

Use sanitizer... touch only clean sides, wash as soon as possible.

20. How do I keep reusable cloths clean if I can’t dry them in the sun?

Air-dry in well-ventilated clean space... ensure fully dry before reuse.
